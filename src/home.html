BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few practical
questions.

Can you work on my type of vehicle?+

We work on commercial trucks, service bodies, hearses, limousines, RVs and other specialty vehicles. Tell us the year, make, model and any custom body or upfit so we can discuss the job.

How long will the repair take?+

Timing depends on the damage, parts availability and the work required. We’ll discuss scheduling after the assessment and keep you informed as the repair progresses.

Will you work with my insurance company?+

Yes. We help with estimates, repair paperwork and coordination with your insurer. If you already have a claim number or estimate, have it available when we talk.

What should I have ready for an estimate?+

Your vehicle details, a description of the damage, and photos of the whole vehicle and affected areas are a useful starting point. An in-person inspection may be needed to establish the repair scope.
