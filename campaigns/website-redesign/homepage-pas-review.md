## Decision

Restore the original downtime-focused Problem–Agitate–Solution argument within the existing approved visual design. This is a homepage rewrite authorized after the copy comparison, not another aesthetic-only pass. Readers are commercial fleet, funeral-home and specialty-vehicle owners who need repair help. Primary action: start an estimate; secondary action: inspect work. Use one cohesive draft for this focused preview rather than alternate body versions.

## Marketing Hub guidance applied

Canonical lowkeycm/Marketing-Hub sources read through authenticated GitHub: website-system/SKILL.md; references/copy-and-claims.md, conversion-architecture.md, website-principles.md; vibe-marketing-skills/skills/direct-response-copy/SKILL.md, calibration anchors and copywriting playbook. Lead with the reader's problem, make consequences concrete, explain the repair mechanism and support the argument with relevant proof and process. Keep meaningful emotional stakes without unsupported promises.

## Page argument

1. Problem: “A vehicle down. A business disrupted.”
2. Agitation: potential missed jobs, disrupted crews and bookings, followed by the difficulty of custom bodies, parts and finishes.
3. Solution: Heritage's commercial collision, specialty body/paint and fabrication capability, followed by service choices.
4. Evidence: real truck comparison and paint-booth photography. These show workmanship, not measured turnaround or business results.
5. Reassurance: estimate, scheduling, insurer coordination, dedicated contact and review of completed repairs.
6. Action: share the vehicle, damage and business need to begin an estimate.
